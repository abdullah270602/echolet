# Echolet

AI voice receptionist for small service businesses (dental clinics, salons, trades) whose
phone rings while their hands are busy.

- **What it does today:** the owner configures an agent (identity, voice, services and
  prices, knowledge text, guardrails, ticket fields). The agent talks to callers in real
  time (browser/WebRTC via LiveKit; no phone numbers yet), saves the transcript, and a
  worker writes a ticket: summary, sentiment, action items.
- **Audience for the landing page:** small-business owners first; technical reviewers second.
- **Landing page job (Persuade):** let a visitor hear the agent within seconds (live demo,
  or a scripted example when the demo is offline), then understand what they'd get after
  a call.
- **Product truths to keep:** no customers, metrics, pricing or testimonials exist; the demo
  clinic (Brightside Dental) is fictional and labelled as such; the example ticket is an
  invented example.
- **Visual world:** the "While You Were Out" phone-message slip. Ballpoint navy ink, slip
  pink, stamp red; Archivo (width axis) for type, a ballpoint scrawl only on the
  handwritten slip. Paper objects have square corners; controls are pills; the phone-line
  panel is the one rounded container.
